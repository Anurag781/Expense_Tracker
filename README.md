# 💰 Minimalist Expense Tracker
**Track your spending. Master your budget.**

The Minimalist Expense Tracker is a streamlined financial tool that helps you monitor your income and expenses in real-time. Designed with a clean interface and offline-first capabilities, it helps you visualize your cash flow without the complexity of traditional banking apps.

---

## ✨ Features
* **Real-Time Balance:** Automatically calculates your total balance, income, and expenses.
* **Transaction History:** A clean, scrollable list of all your recent activity.
* **Smart Categorization:** Easily distinguish between "Plus" (Income) and "Minus" (Expense) entries.
* **Offline Persistence:** Uses LocalStorage to save your data so it’s still there when you refresh the page.
* **Responsive Design:** Works perfectly on desktop, tablet, and mobile devices.

## 🛠 Tech Stack
* **HTML5:** Semantic structure for financial data.
* **CSS:** For a modern, professional, and responsive UI.
* **JavaScript:** Logic for calculations, DOM manipulation, and data persistence.
